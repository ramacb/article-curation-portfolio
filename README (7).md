# Article Curation Tool & Workflow

Welcome to my first project repository! This repository showcases my workflow, structural frameworks, and formatting templates for professional **Article Curation** and **Digital Content Research**.

## 📋 What is Article Curation?
Article curation is the process of discovering, filtering, and compiling high-value external resources on a specific industry topic and formatting them into readable, engaging blog round-ups or email newsletters.

## 🛠️ Included Assets
* `curation_framework.md` — A step-by-step master checklist for discovering high-quality sources, verifying accuracy, and extracting value.
* `newsletter_template.md` — A plug-and-play markdown layout for generating structured link round-ups.
